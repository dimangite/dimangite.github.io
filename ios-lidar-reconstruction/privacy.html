LiDAR Reconstruction Privacy Policy

Effective: October 15, 2026

What the app uses

LiDAR Reconstruction uses the iPhone camera and ARKit imagery during capture, together with LiDAR/depth information and device motion/tracking signals, to build 3D reconstructions and present scan-quality information.

Local storage

Saved scans and room captures are stored in the app's local container on the device. The app does not require an account to create or keep these captures.

Export and sharing

The app exports scan files only when you choose an export or share action. A selected share destination may transmit the exported file according to that destination's own behavior and privacy terms.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current shipping app does not include an advertising SDK, account/profile system, analytics SDK, or third-party crash-reporting SDK. It does not implement an automatic server upload path for scan data.

Deletion

You can delete individual saved captures from the app and can use the Settings storage controls to delete all app-managed saved scans and rooms. Files you previously exported or shared are not removed by deleting the app's local copies.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the shipping app's data practices change. The policy should be read together with the behavior of the installed app version.

Support

For questions or issues, use the project's public support tracker: GitHub Issues.
